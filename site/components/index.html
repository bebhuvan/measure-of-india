What it does not say

States the limits of the number and, where honest, says we do not know.

07 · Explain pair — .explain
Under every plate. Left: evergreen reading protocol. Right (accent kicker): the dated reading. Both must hold long text.
How to read this

Timeless. Never references "this month". Teaches the grammar of the chart: what the lines answer, what the band means, what to ignore.

As of January 2026

Dated. Names its date, interprets the current configuration, and says what would change the picture.

08 · Margin note — .margin-note
Rail notes beside prose in §05. The sharpest one-liners on the page.
Margin note · i Falling inflation ≠ falling prices. The price level almost never returns. A falling rate is slower pain, not relief.
09 · Misread item — .misread li
Roman-numbered failure modes. The mistake quoted as the heading; the correction bolds its key phrase.
  • i.

    "Inflation fell, so prices fell."

    No. Falling inflation means prices are rising more slowly — disinflation. Only a negative print means falling prices.

10 · Definition box — .defbox
For technical terms on learn pages. Ink border with overlapping mono tab, Archivo term with mono qualifier, Newsreader definition, italic plain-words example over a hairline.
Disinflation the rate falling, still positive

Prices rising more slowly than before. The rate comes down; the price level does not.

In plain words: the pain is slowing, not reversing.
