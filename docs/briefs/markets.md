# Brief: markets, savings and companies (`markets`, port 4435)

Read `dbie-wave.md` first. A new guide on where Indian savings go outside banks and how
Indian companies are doing. Inventory items R11, R12, R13.

Reader questions: How much do companies borrow from markets (commercial paper, bonds) rather
than banks? How big have mutual funds become, and who puts money in? How much do companies
raise through new issues? How are private and listed companies doing on sales, profits and
debt (RBI's company finance studies)? What do professional forecasters and households
expect for inflation and growth?

Sources: CP 5, 32 and CD 4, 31; corporate debt and capital issues 1355; mutual funds 1359 to
1361; gold and silver prices 17 (Data Bank). Company finance studies 1146 to 1208 (Data Bank).
The Survey of Professional Forecasters (1475, 1476) via the RBI Innovation Hub service; the
household inflation expectations and consumer confidence surveys (IESH 271, UCCS 272, RCCS
1478) if either source has them, otherwise `blocked` with the reason. Stock-exchange index
levels reproduced by RBI (693, 728) are BSE/NSE data: exclude them ("exchange-owned index
data; licence not ours to republish"). FPI flows live in `external`: link.

Seams: SEBI's mutual-fund category recast (2018); company study samples change every year
(a study's growth rates compare the same companies; never chain levels across studies).
