Four directions.

Same dossier · same data · four temperaments
H

The Monograph, elevated

The first direction, finished. A's quiet single-accent voice with everything learned since: narrow prose with wide breakout plates, full axes, crosshair, downloads, ledger sparklines, margin notes, explanation boxes.

 → G

Avant

Radical scale and asymmetry. Expanded grotesk at 12vw, a cropped 23rem stat, black full-bleed interludes, outlined ghost numerals, one Klein blue accent. Charts with a side annotation column instead of boxes.

 → F

The Register

Built from the pitfalls of E: the Indian bahi-khata ledger as design language. Double red-ruled folio margin, Bodoni monumental numerals, Devanagari accents, a review stamp, a basket-weights bar, charts on surveyor's ruling.

 → E

The Synthesis — A + D

Monograph's restraint, Archive's warmth, on pure white. Wide layout, a ledger with live sparklines, an RBI-band main plate, margin notes, and a 4-across component gallery.

 → A

The Monograph

A scholarly paper edition. Warm paper, serif prose, one vermillion accent, numbered sections and figure plates. Reads like a beautiful RBI annual report essay.

 → B

The Instrument

Swiss-modernist precision. Hairline grid, tabular numerals, dossier ID and review status, small multiples, a numbered "reading protocol". Data as laboratory equipment.

 → C

The Night Desk

The contrasting dark edition. Deep ink ground, luminous chart lines in saffron, aqua and rose, giant serif numerals, field notes on how the number lies.

 → D

The Archive

An Indian almanac. Cream laid paper, chapter tabs, ledger tables, margin notes and a review stamp — terracotta, indigo, leaf and mustard. Warm and authoritative.
