How to use this site

Start with a dossier's current reading if you came for the news; read it end to end if you came to understand. Every chart downloads as SVG, PNG or CSV, and every number on the page is computed from the same data the charts use.

What we promise

Every observation traceable to its source. Derived series labelled as derived. Conflicting statistics shown side by side, never reconciled. And where something is not measured, the page says we do not know.

India Atlas

A static-first reference work for Indian economic statistics. Published continuously; each dossier carries its vintage and revision history. Data shown is illustrative while in design preview.

Sources

MoSPI / NSO for prices and national accounts; Office of the Economic Adviser for wholesale prices. Each dossier cites its exact press release and release date.
