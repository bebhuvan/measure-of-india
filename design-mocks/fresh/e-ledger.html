    Consumer prices

    Every month the statistics office prices a few hundred things that households buy and reduces them to one number. In June that number rose for the fifth month running.

    Latest4.38% in June 2026
    Released12 July 2026
    SourceNational Statistics Office
    Base2024 = 100

    Prices were 4.38 per cent higher in June than a year earlier, up from 3.94 per cent in May and 2.73 per cent in January. Food and beverages climbed 5.06 per cent and eating out 6.91 per cent, while transport jumped from 1.75 per cent in May to 4.31 per cent in June. Villages feel it more than towns, and the spread across states is wide. The headline is now above the RBI's 4 per cent target, though still well inside its band of 2 to 6 per cent.
