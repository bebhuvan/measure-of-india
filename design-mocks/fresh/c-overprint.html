    Dossier on consumer prices

    Prices are rising again

    Retail inflation has climbed every month this year and crossed the RBI's 4% target in June. Among everyday spending, food and eating out are rising fastest.

    Consumer Price Index, base 2024. June 2026 figures, released by the NSO on 12 July 2026. Monthly, all India.

    Inflation since 2014

    Year-on-year change in the headline index. Hover to read any month.

    The lighter line before 2025 is the NSO's back-cast: its estimate of how the new 2024 basket would have moved in earlier years. From January 2025 the line is live, collected prices. No figure from the retired 2012 series is mixed in.

    What the print says

    Retail inflation rose to 4.38% in June, from 3.94% in May. It is the fifth rise in a row since January, when the year opened at 2.73%, and the first reading above the 4% target since January 2025. The rise is real but not yet broad. Food and beverages, the heaviest part of the basket, climbed to 5.06%. Eating out ran hotter still at 6.91%. Transport, flat through April, jumped to 4.31% in two months. Villages feel it more than towns: rural inflation is 4.74% against 3.93% in urban areas. Housing, health and phone bills are still rising slowly, which is why the headline sits nearer the target than the grocery bill suggests.
