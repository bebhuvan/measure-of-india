Consumer prices

Every month the statistics office prices a few hundred things that households buy and reduces them to one number. In June that number rose for the fifth month running.

Prices were 4.38 per cent higher in June than a year earlier, up from 3.94 per cent in May and 2.73 per cent in January. The rise is real but not yet broad. Food and beverages climbed 5.06 per cent and eating out 6.91 per cent, while transport jumped from 1.75 per cent in May to 4.31 per cent in June.

Villages feel it more than towns: rural prices rose 4.74 per cent against 3.93 per cent in urban India. Across states the spread runs from 1.62 per cent in Mizoram to 6.36 per cent in Telangana. The headline is above the RBI's 4 per cent target, and still well inside its band of 2 to 6 per cent.

Below zero is sea: in those months output was lower than a year before.
