The Atlas chart kit

Nineteen forms, one grammar, drawn from the Atlas's own data. Each chart below is real: the finding in the title is computed from the series it shows.

  • The finding is the title. A sentence a reader could repeat. The measure, unit and period go underneath.
  • Labels sit on the data. Values at line ends and bar tips; a key only when labels cannot reach.
  • One colour carries the story. Indigo for the series that matters, sindoor for the one comparison, greys for context.
  • Indian numbers. 12,34,567 and lakh crore; a true minus sign; figures that align in columns.
  • Gaps stay gaps. A missing month is a break. A new base is a seam, drawn and named.
  • No chrome. No boxes, no vertical gridlines, no legend you have to match by colour.
Specimen for the India Atlas, October 2026. Data: the Atlas's committed bundles (MoSPI, RBI, Ember, PLFS). Kit: design-mocks/chartkit/kit.js.
