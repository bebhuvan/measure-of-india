In 2021-23 the Sample Registration System counted 917 girls born for every 1,000 boys, up from 898 in 2014-16, and recorded 88 maternal deaths per 1,00,000 live births, against 254 in 2004-06. Girls no longer die in infancy more often than boys. In 2023 the rate was 25 deaths per 1,000 births for girls and 26 for boys, and life expectancy at birth in 2019-23 is 72.5 years for women against 68.5 for men. Schooling divides fertility as sharply as anything measured here: 3.3 children per woman among mothers who never went to school, 1.6 among graduates.

In PLFS 2025, 40% of women aged 15 and over were in the labour force, against 79.1% of men. The Time Use Survey found women spending 305 minutes a day on unpaid work at home in 2024 and men 56, and women's figure has not moved since 2019 while men's fell from 67. A woman in rural casual work earns 305 rupees a day where a man earns 435, about 70 rupees for every 100. Women hold 13.63% of the seats in the Lok Sabha after the 2024 election and 49.75% of panchayat seats in 2025, and the NCRB registered 66.2 crimes against women per 1,00,000 women in 2023.
