The 2025 edition of Women and Men in India collects figures from different agencies, each on its own calendar. The Sample Registration System counts 917 girls born for every 1,000 boys in 2021-23, against 898 in 2014-16. Girls' infant deaths were 25 per 1,000 births in 2023, a shade below the 26 for boys, and maternal deaths were 88 per 1,00,000 live births in 2021-23, against 254 in 2004-06. Life expectancy at birth in 2019-23 was 72.5 years for women and 68.5 for men, and family size tracks schooling: 3.3 children for a mother who never went to school, 1.6 for a graduate.

In PLFS 2025, 40.0% of women aged 15 and over were in the labour force, against 79.1% of men. The Time Use Survey puts women's unpaid work at home at 305 minutes a day in 2024, the same as in 2019; men's 56 minutes is down from 67. On a rural casual wage a woman earned ₹305 a day in 2025 and a man ₹435, so she took home 70 rupees for every 100 he did. Women hold just under half of panchayat seats in 2025 and 13.7% of the Lok Sabha elected in 2024; the National Crime Records Bureau counted 66.2 crimes against women per 1,00,000 women in 2023, a count of reports, not offences.
