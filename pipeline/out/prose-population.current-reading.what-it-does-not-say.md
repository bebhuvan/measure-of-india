India's population on 1 July 2023 is put at 143.81 crore. That is not a count, but it is not a guess. India has not taken a census since 2011, so the figure is the UN's model, built from the 2011 census, the sample registration of births and deaths, and surveys. The UN re-estimates the whole history every two years, so the number for 2023 can change without anything in India having changed. A single national figure averages over states that differ widely, and India's own official estimates can differ from the UN's. How far the model is from the count the next census will produce, we do not know.
