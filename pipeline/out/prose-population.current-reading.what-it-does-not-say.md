The 2023 figure is the UN's model, not a count, but it is the best comparable figure we have. India has not taken a census since 2011, so the estimate is built from that census, from sample registration of births and deaths, and from surveys. The next census will revise it, and the UN re-estimates the whole history every two years. India's own official figures can differ because they count differently. A national total of about 143.8 crore hides states that are still adding people quickly and states that are barely adding any. We do not know the number to the nearest crore.
