The repo rate moved up a quarter point to 5.50% in October 2026, and the corridor moved with it: the SDF to 5.25%, the MSF and the Bank Rate to 5.75%. The cash reserve ratio held at 3%, half a point below a year ago, and the SLR at 18%; the fixed reverse repo has sat at 3.35% all year. A loan priced off the repo resets within three months; one priced off a bank's own lending rate may wait a year, and the bank rates in this sheet were struck for August, before the decision, so they cannot show its effect yet: private banks' outstanding loans at 9.82%, fresh foreign-bank deposits at 4.7%. The minutes, with each member's reasons, come about two weeks after the meeting. Real rates subtract the inflation that has happened, 4.82% year on year, not what savers expected; the real call money rate is 0.33%, and we do not know how quickly banks will pass the change on.
