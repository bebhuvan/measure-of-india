The repo rate is 5.50% in October 2026. The figure alone does not say when your loan will change: a home loan priced off the repo rate resets within about three months, while one priced off the bank's own lending rate may wait a year. The lending and deposit rates in this guide were struck in August, before the decision, and the cash reserve ratio has not moved either, so neither can yet show what October did. Real rates subtract the inflation that has already happened, not what savers expected when they opened a fixed deposit. The minutes, with each member's reasons, come about two weeks after the meeting, and we do not know how fast banks will pass the change on.
