The general index rose 8.0% in August 2026 from a year earlier. It is one month, and a month is noise: Capital Goods ran at 16.9%, Mining & Quarrying at -5.6% against 15.8% a year earlier, and the gap between the fastest line and the slowest is 22.5 points. Four of the eleven lines moved against the headline: Water Supply at 6.3%, below its 6.9% a year ago; Infrastructure and Construction Goods at 6.4%, below 9.2%; Primary Goods at 3.5%, below 7.6%; and Mining, the weakest of the eleven. Intermediate Goods grew 13.7%, Electricity & Gas Supply 12.3%, Consumer Durables 11.1%, Manufacturing 9.0% and Consumer Non-durables 2.1%, all quick estimates that later releases revise. And the index counts goods only: services, more than half the economy, live in a different index, while the 32 industries beneath the aggregates run from Manufacture of Electrical Equipment at 30.9% to Non-Metallic Minerals at -12.8%, with eight of them below zero. We do not know how much of this month survives revision.
