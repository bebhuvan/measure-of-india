The general index for August 2026 reads 7.97%, 0.6 points above July and 3.3 points above a year ago. It is a month, and a month is noise: holidays, a plant shutdown, one heavy monsoon week move it more than any underlying trend. These are quick estimates, revised in later releases, and the index counts goods only: services, more than half the economy, live in a different index. Four of the eleven lines moved against the headline, mining contracting 5.62%, the weakest on the sheet and 21.4 points below its reading a year ago, while water supply, infrastructure and construction goods, and primary goods all slowed. Capital goods is the hottest line at 16.86%, and beneath it the 32 industries range from electrical equipment at 30.92% down to non-metallic minerals at -12.76%, with a median of 8.62%: 17 accelerated against the previous month, 15 slowed, and eight contracted outright. We do not know the true monthly pulse until the revisions settle.
