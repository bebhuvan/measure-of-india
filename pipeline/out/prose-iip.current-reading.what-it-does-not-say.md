Industrial output rose 7.97% in Aug 2026. The headline is one month, and the lines beneath it disagree: capital goods at 16.86% against mining at -5.62%, a spread of 22.5 points, with primary goods, infrastructure and construction goods and water supply all slower than a year ago even as the general index rose. Deeper in, 32 industries run from electrical equipment at 30.92% down to non-metallic minerals at -12.76%, 17 rising against 15 falling and 8 below zero. A single plant shutdown or a shift in the festival calendar can move the index more than any underlying trend does. These are quick estimates, revised in later releases, and they count goods only: services live in a different index. We do not know the true monthly pulse until the revisions settle.
