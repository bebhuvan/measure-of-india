The 2024 estimate puts female participation at 32.41%, and for most of the years behind that line the number is the model's arithmetic, not a survey's count. India did run surveys through much of that span, the NSS rounds and then the PLFS; the ILO marks only a few years as taken directly from one, so a modelled year is not a year in which no survey happened. Each annual edition rewrites the whole history backward, which makes a revision a new estimate of the past rather than news about it. The ILO counts work over a week and by calendar year; MoSPI's headline counts it over a year, July to June, so the two never agree and should never be joined. How much of women's work is counted at all depends on what the survey calls work. We do not know how much of the recent rise is new paid work and how much is unpaid help on family farms.
