Each figure here belongs to the agency that measured it and to that agency's own calendar, so the Sample Registration System's numbers arrive on different spans: 917 girls born per 1,000 boys for 2021-23, 88 maternal deaths per 1,00,000 live births for the same years, 25 infant deaths per 1,000 births for girls against 26 for boys in 2023, and life expectancy of 72.5 years for women against 68.5 for men over the five years to 2023. Recorded crime is what the police registered, not what happened: 66.2 crimes against women per 1,00,000 women in 2023, 133.6 in Delhi against 5.2 in Nagaland, and a state's rate can rise with more reporting rather than more crime. An account in a woman's name is not money she controls: women hold 55.8% of Jan Dhan accounts but 40.3% of the value of individual deposits. We do not know how many women decide how that money is spent. The 2025 labour figures come from a survey whose design changed that year, so they cannot be joined to earlier rounds: 40.0% of women aged 15 and over are in the labour force against 79.1% of men, and rural casual work pays women ₹305 a day against ₹435 for men. There has been no census since 2011, so 943 women per 1,000 men is a count from that year, and the remaining measures describe one thing each and cannot be added together: 49.8% of panchayat members are women against 13.7% of Lok Sabha members, unpaid work at home runs 305 minutes a day for women against 56 for men, and mothers who never went to school have 3.3 children per woman against 1.6 for graduates.
