Wholesale prices stood 9.92% above a year earlier in Aug 2026, barely moved from July's 9.78%. Fuel and power did the lifting: 22.93%, up from 20.05%, a jump of 2.9 points in a single month and the hottest reading on the sheet. Primary articles ran at 7.76%, easing from 8.52%, while manufactured products edged up to 8.37% from 8.29%, a ninth straight monthly rise since Nov 2025 and the smallest year-on-year move of the five, at 6.3 points. Food prices rose to 7.05% from 6.65%, the tenth straight increase since Oct 2025. Beneath the major groups the spread is wide: lignite at 62.42% and mineral oils at 38.48% at the top, electricity at -1.73% and magnetic and optical media at -14.59% at the bottom; electricity fell on the month while the headline climbed. At the item level, 513 of the 953 rose against 414 that fell, 141 sat below zero, and the range runs from sulphur at 303.87% down to tomato at -40.2%. Part of the year-on-year size is arithmetic: a year earlier the headline print was 0.00%, and primary, fuel and food were all negative, so this month's climb is measured from a low base.
