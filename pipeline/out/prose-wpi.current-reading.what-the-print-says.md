Wholesale prices rose 8.36% in April, the sixth straight monthly climb and the fastest reading since the run began in October. The jump is real, but it is not broad. Fuel and power — barely a tenth of the basket — surged 24.71%, a single bound that carried the headline. Primary articles added 9.17%, their own acceleration. The quiet majority, manufactured goods, rose 4.62%: warm, steady, and far from alarming. Food is the one part moving against the headline: the food index sits at 2.31%, its y/y easing 0.99 points while everything else firms. The flattery expires with the next energy print.
