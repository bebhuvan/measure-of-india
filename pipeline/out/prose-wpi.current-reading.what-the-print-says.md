The wholesale print for Aug 2026 is 9.92%, up from 9.78% in July and a shade under the 9.97% it touched in June. Fuel carried it: 22.93%, the highest rate among the five groups, up 2.88 points on the month and 26.01 points on a year ago. The rest did not follow in step: primary articles eased to 7.76%, down 0.76 points, the only major group to fall while the headline rose. Manufactured products, the quiet two-thirds, rose to 8.37%: a ninth straight monthly rise, but a move of only 0.08 points and a year-on-year gap of 6.34 points, the smallest of the five. Food is the slow climber at 7.05%, a tenth consecutive rise, and much of the headline's height is arithmetic: a year ago primary stood at -3.69% and fuel at -3.08%. The two-digit groups run from Mineral Oils at 38.48% down to Electricity at -1.73%, and the 142 sub-groups from Lignite at 62.42% to Manufacture Of Magnetic And Optical Media at -14.59%. Among the 953 items the median rate is 5.87%, with 513 rates faster than last month against 414 slower and 141 below zero; the extremes are Sulphur at 303.87% and Tomato at -40.2%, and the eight sub-sub-groups run from Fruits at 8.18% to Non-Coking Coal G15 to G17 at -7.82%.
