The economy grew 7.83% in Q4 FY26, easing from 7.96% in the previous quarter. That is the second straight quarter of deceleration, though the pace remains well above the 6.98% of a year ago. The composition is the encouraging part: consumption rose 7.06%, and investment jumped to 10.82% — its fastest print in the series. GVA came in at 7.88%, slightly above GDP. The gap is a net-tax story, not a production story.
