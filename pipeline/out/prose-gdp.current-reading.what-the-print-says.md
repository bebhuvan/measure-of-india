GDP grew 7.8% in Q1 FY27, down from 8.6% in Q4 FY26, a fall of 0.8 points. Consumption rose 7.1% and investment 12.0%, the hottest of the four headline series, and investment has now risen for four straight prints, since Q1 FY26. GVA grew 8.2%, faster than GDP, and that gap is net taxes, not production. Underneath, the spread is wider still: exports grew 12.1% and finance & real estate 12.1%, while imports fell 1.1% and mining & quarrying shrank 2.4%, with utilities the group that sped up most, to 8.9%. Nominal GDP grew 10.3% and the deflator 2.3%. In the annual accounts, the gross saving rate was 34.8% of GDP, capital formation grew 8.1%, and subsidies sped up most among those lines, to 7.7%.
