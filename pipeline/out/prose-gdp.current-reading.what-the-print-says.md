GDP grew 8.23% in Q2 FY26, up from 7.81% in Q1 FY26. Consumption rose 7.95%, accelerating from 7.05% last quarter. Investment eased to 7.33% from 7.82%. GVA came in at 8.12%, slightly below GDP — the gap is a net-tax story, not a production story. The quarter was carried by domestic demand, with consumption outpacing investment for the first time in two quarters.
