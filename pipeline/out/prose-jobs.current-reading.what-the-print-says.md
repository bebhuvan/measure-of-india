Unemployment was 5.00% in Aug 2026, down 0.1 points from July and 0.1 points from a year earlier. The cooling is genuine, and small: the rate has stayed between 4.7% and 5.6% through the monthly bulletin's short life, so a tenth of a point is as much noise as news. Participation and work moved the other way: 55.60% and 52.80%, up 0.2 and 0.3 points on July, and 0.6 points each on Aug 2025. More people joined the pool and more were counted as working; that is what a falling rate beside a rising participation rate looks like. In the gender-and-sector cut the spread is wide: rural men's participation is the highest reading at 78.40%, urban women's the lowest at 25.40% and the largest faller on the year, down 0.7 points, while rural women's participation rose 2.0 points to 39.40%. The lowest unemployment reading in that cut is rural women at 3.90%. The annual cuts, older vintages, carry the rest: among states in 2023-24 the median rate was 4.8%, with 42 rising against 32 falling, Lakshadweep the highest at 18.3% and Madhya Pradesh the lowest at 1.0%; among worker statuses, self-employed women are the biggest group at 67.4% and regular-wage women the smallest at 15.9%, with casual work by women down 2.1 points to 16.7%; and earnings still sit on three denominators, ₹22,375 a month for regular-wage men against ₹306.4 a day for casual women.
