In Aug 2026 the unemployment rate was 5.00%, down 0.1 points from July and 0.1 points from a year earlier. Participation stood at 55.6% and the worker ratio at 52.8%, each 0.6 points above Aug 2025. Unemployment falls both when people find work and when they stop looking; this print is the first kind, with more people joining the labour force and more of them finding work, which is why participation and the worker ratio rose as the rate fell. Rural men's participation is the highest reading in the gender-and-sector cut, at 78.4%; rural women's unemployment is the lowest, at 3.9%. The young carry more of it: in the quarterly cut, rural women aged 15 to 29 face 17.1%, up 4.7 points on the year, and the share of young rural people working fell 2.3 points to 34.5%. Counting urban women of all ages rather than 15 and above, participation was 20.4%, the age cut's largest fall on the year, down 0.6 points. We do not know what these jobs pay.
