The unemployment rate held at 5.5% in June 2026, unchanged from May and a tenth of a point below a year earlier. Participation also sat still, at 54.4%. The share of people in the labour pool has drifted down from 55.9% in January, so the flat headline hides a slow retreat from the workforce. Urban women still carry the highest unemployment of any labour market; rural men the lowest. The cooling is genuine but modest — the rate has not strayed far from the 5.1% to 5.5% band of the last four months. We do not know whether the drop from last June is jobs found or people gone.
