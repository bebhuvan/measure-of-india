In August 2026 the unemployment rate was 5.00%, down 0.1 points from July and 0.1 points from a year earlier. Participation rose to 55.6% and the worker population ratio to 52.8%, each up 0.6 points on a year earlier. Both moved against the headline, and the arithmetic behind that is plain: more people joined the labour force, and more of them found work. Among the gender and sector cuts, rural men hold the highest participation reading at 78.4% and rural women the lowest unemployment reading at 3.9%, while rural women's participation rose 2.0 points on a year ago to 39.4% and urban women's fell 0.7 points to 25.4%. In the age cuts, rural men aged 15 to 29 recorded the highest participation, 61.1%, and rural women aged 15 to 29 carried a 17.1% unemployment rate in the April to June 2026 quarter, up 4.7 points on a year earlier. The whole sheet sits on the revamped design that began in January 2025, so nothing here can be set against a year before that. We do not know what the jobs pay.
