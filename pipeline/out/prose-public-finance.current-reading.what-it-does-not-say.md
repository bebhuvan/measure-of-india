By July 2026 the Centre had used 26.84% of the fiscal deficit its budget allows for the whole year. These are unaudited accounts, added up from April, and the deficit does not arrive evenly: taxes come in quarterly instalments, the RBI's dividend usually lands in May, and much of the spending waits for the last months, close to March. So the share used by now does not say how the year will end. The budget is a plan written before the year began and revised in February, and these figures cover the Centre alone; the states keep their own books. We do not know where the year's deficit will finally land.
