Services made up 54.03% of gross value added in FY26, the provisional estimate, up 1.15 points from 52.88% in FY25 and the third straight annual rise since FY23. Finance, real estate and professional services is the largest of the three groups behind it, at 26.55%, up 0.55 points; trade, hotels, transport and communication is 14.31%, and public administration, defence and other services 13.18%. Goods came to 45.97%, down 1.15 points; the two add to 100 by construction, and neither explains the other. The sub-industry detail stops at FY25: storage grew fastest in real terms, 28.3%; railways slowest, 2.5%; hotels and restaurants slowed most, to 3.9%, down 11.0 points on a year earlier. Services trade for May 2026 ran to $33.4 billion of exports and $17.6 billion of imports. The trial Index of Services Production, 19 sub-sectors, ran from administrative and support services at 20.8% year on year in July 2026 down to air transport at -8.5%. The share is provisional and will be revised, and the sub-industry figures run a year behind it; whether the small informal firm grew as fast as the listed one, we do not know.
