The headline for Aug 2026 is 4.8%. December 2025's 1.3% is the retired 2012 base's last reading: a different ruler, not an earlier step on the same one. The 2024 base publishes no component weights, so no core index can be computed on it; the final core figure, 4.6%, is a 2012-base number, measured in a month whose headline was 1.3%. What the new base does show is spread: food and beverages at 5.7%, restaurants and accommodation services at 8.4%, personal care at 15.2%, and health at 1.3%, the lowest of the twelve divisions. What it cannot show is whether underlying pressure is easing, because the instrument for that question does not exist here. We do not know.
