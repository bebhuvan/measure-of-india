The August 2026 print is 4.82%, and it carries a limit worth stating plainly: the 2024 basket publishes no component weights, so no core index can be computed on it. The only core reading on the sheet is retired, 4.63% in December 2025, on the 2012 base, a month whose headline was 1.34%. That is not a jump from 4.82%. The two come from different rulers, and the distance between them is arithmetic, not a price move. We do not know what core inflation is now. Read the twelve divisions one at a time instead: personal care at 15.18% and health at 1.33% are both true, and without weights nobody can say which pulls the average harder.
