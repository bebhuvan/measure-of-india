The June headline of 4.38% is the fifth straight rise, but it is not a jump from the 1.34% of December. Those are different rulers. The June figure is measured on the 2024 base; the December figure belongs to the retired 2012 base. The new base serves no component weights, so no core index can be computed on it — the last core reading, 4.63%, lives on the old ruler. We do not know how much of the gap is real price pressure and how much is the new arithmetic. The two prints will only speak to each other once a year of the new base has elapsed.
