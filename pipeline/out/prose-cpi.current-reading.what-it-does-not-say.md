The December print of 1.34% is the calmest year-end reading in this series, but it does not say prices are falling — the basket is still dearer than a year ago. Nor does it say underlying pressure has eased: core inflation, at 4.63%, has climbed through the year and now sits more than three points above the headline. When core exceeds headline, the average is being held down by volatile items, and that configuration is stable only until the next weather shock. The cooling is genuine but unevenly distributed, almost entirely a food story. We do not know which way 2026 breaks; the next two prints will say.
