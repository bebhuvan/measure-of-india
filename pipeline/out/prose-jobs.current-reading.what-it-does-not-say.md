August 2026 puts the unemployment rate at 5.00%, a tenth of a point below July and a tenth below a year ago, and that does not say jobs were created. The rate is a ratio; it can fall because more people found work, or because more stopped looking and left the pool altogether. Participation moved against it, to 55.6%, up 0.6 points on the year, and the worker share to 52.8%, also up 0.6: the pool grew while the headline fell. The bulletin is young, with no seasonal history to adjust against, and it counts work done last week, not a pay slip. Under it the cuts spread wide: rural female participation at 39.4% and up 2.0 points on the year, urban female at 25.4% and down 0.7; 67.4% of working women self-employed against 15.9% in regular wage work; state rates from 18.3% in Lakshadweep down to 1.0% in Madhya Pradesh, median 4.8%, with 42 rising against 32 falling; and average earnings on three separate denominators, ₹22,375 a month for regular wage men and ₹306.4 a day for casual women. We do not know how many of these jobs pay a living wage.
