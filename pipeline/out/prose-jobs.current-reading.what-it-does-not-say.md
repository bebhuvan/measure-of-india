The unemployment rate for August 2026 was 5.00%, down 0.1 points from July and 0.1 points from a year earlier. It is a ratio, and a ratio can fall for the wrong reason: people leaving the labour force rather than finding work. That is not what happened in August, since participation and the worker population ratio each stood 0.6 points above their year-ago readings, and the same bulletin reports them by gender, sector and age. The bulletin is young, and the survey was redesigned in January 2025, so there is no seasonal history to adjust against and nothing here can be set beside an earlier year. It counts work done last week, not a pay slip. We do not know how many of the jobs it counts pay enough to live on.
