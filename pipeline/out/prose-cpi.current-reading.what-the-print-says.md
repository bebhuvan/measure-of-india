Retail inflation rose to 4.38% in June, the fifth straight monthly increase since the series opened in January at 2.73%. The print sits 0.38 points above the 4% target. The rise is genuine but not yet broad: food and beverages, the heaviest weight in the basket, climbed to 5.06%, and transport jumped 2.56 points to 4.31% — its sharpest move of the year. Restaurants and accommodation, at 6.91%, have more than doubled since February, while at the floor information and communication sits at 0.42%, phones and data getting cheaper as everything else firms. One division is a spectator: personal care, at 16.73%, carries gold, whose price is set in world markets, not in Indian shops. The firming is real, but it is still a five-month-old series. We do not know whether this is a trend or a base effect finding its footing.
