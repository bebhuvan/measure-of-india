Retail inflation in December 2025 printed at 1.34%, up from 0.75% in November. The reading is the fourth consecutive month below 2%. Food and beverages, which account for 45.9% of the basket, fell 1.85% from a year ago — the sixth month of food deflation in the last seven. Fuel rose 1.97%, a mild contribution in either direction.

The calm is genuine but narrow. The headline has climbed from its October trough of 0.29%, yet the move is a food story unwinding, not a broad repricing.
