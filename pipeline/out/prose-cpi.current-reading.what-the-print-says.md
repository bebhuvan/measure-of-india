Retail inflation on the new 2024 base was 4.82% in Aug 2026, the seventh straight rise since the series opened at 2.73% in January, and it sits 0.82 points above the 4% target. Food and beverages is at 5.66% and has climbed every month this year; transport is at 4.60%; restaurants and accommodation, at 8.38%, has spent three months above the 6% ceiling. Personal care is highest at 15.18%, but that is the division that carries gold, and gold's price is set in world markets.

The firming is genuine. But it is narrower than the headline: nine of the thirteen divisions print below it, health at 1.33% has fallen for seven straight months and sat under the 2% floor since February, and paan, tobacco and intoxicants has eased three months running to 4.70%. Below the divisions the spread widens: among 358 items, silver jewellery is at 107.12% and tomato at -31.09%; across 43 groups the median is 2.44%, with 32 rising against 10 falling; states run from Telangana at 6.27% to Mizoram at 2.36%, or 6.79% to 1.98% once split rural and urban.
