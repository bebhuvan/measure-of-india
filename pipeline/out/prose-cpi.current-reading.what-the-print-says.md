Headline inflation was 4.82% in Aug 2026. That is up from 4.45% in July and 2.73% in January, seven straight rises, and 0.82 points above the 4% target. Food and beverages, at 5.66%, and transport, at 4.60%, both rose again; restaurants and accommodation services, at 8.38%, has been above the 6% ceiling for three months. The highest rate on the sheet is personal care, at 15.18%, and it carries gold: that price is set in world markets, so it is a spectator, not a domestic story. Health went the other way, at 1.33%, down for a seventh straight print and below the 2% floor for a seventh; paan, tobacco and intoxicants, at 4.70%, has slipped three prints running.

Rural inflation is 5.23% against urban 4.31%, and the spread across states and divisions runs from Ladakh's paan, tobacco and intoxicants at 38.07% to Goa's at -3.74%, median 3.17%. The Labour Bureau's industrial-worker index, a separate instrument for the same month, has its six groups running from food at 6.26% to clothing, bedding and footwear at 3.60%; its general rate belongs to the §13 plate.
