On the 2024 base, headline inflation was 4.8% in Aug 2026, up 0.3 points from July's 4.5%, 0.8 points above the 4% target, and the seventh straight rise since January's 2.7%. Food and beverages ran at 5.7% and transport at 4.6%; restaurants and accommodation services, at 8.4%, spent a third month above the 6% ceiling; and the highest of the twelve divisions, personal care at 15.2%, carries gold, which answers to world prices: a spectator rather than a domestic story. The climb is genuine; its breadth is not: three divisions sit above the headline and nine below, health, the lowest at 1.3%, has been under the 2% floor for seven prints, and only paan, tobacco and intoxicants moved against the print, down 0.1 points. Rural inflation was 5.2% against urban 4.3%, and inside each the pattern repeats: personal care at 16.0% rural and 14.1% urban, health at 1.3% in both. State indices run from Telangana at 6.3% to Mizoram at 2.4%, their rural and urban versions from Telangana rural at 6.8% to Mizoram urban at 2.0%, and the state-by-division tables widen the range to Ladakh's paan, tobacco and intoxicants at 38.1% and Goa's at -3.7%. The 358 items run from silver jewellery at 107.1% to tomato at -31.1%, and the 43 groups from other personal effects at 44.6% to purchase of vehicles at -4.4%. The Labour Bureau's separate index for industrial workers, for the same month, holds its six groups between food at 6.3% and clothing, bedding and footwear at 3.6%; its general rate is left to the §13 plate.
