The 2023-24 survey gives averages: rural India at ₹4,122 a month, urban at ₹6,996, and, with free welfare goods counted in, ₹4,247 and ₹7,078. Those imputed rupees were never paid by any household. The survey publishes no median, so we do not know what the middle household spends, and a mean drifts above a median wherever the top of the ladder sits far from the rest. Spending is not income, and the Gini here, 0.284 in urban India, measures consumption alone; of 144 state Ginis, 128 fell. This is two survey rounds, not a monthly pulse. The fractile table is slices of one distribution, not rungs of one ladder: 95-100% is the richest twentieth of urban households, at ₹20,310 a month, and 0-5% rural is the poorest twentieth, at ₹1,677.
