Bad loans kept falling at India's banks. In the quarter ending March 2026 the gross NPA ratio for all scheduled commercial banks was 1.82%, against 2.31% a year earlier and 11.46% at the March 2018 peak. Public sector banks were at 2.11%, down for 19 quarters running, and private banks at 1.48%; after provisions, all banks together were at 0.42%. Small finance banks, which lend to the smallest borrowers, carry the most bad loans, 3.09%, and foreign banks the fewest, 0.56% gross and 0.09% net.

Public sector banks hold capital equal to 16.59% of their risk-weighted assets, 0.5 points more than a year earlier. Returns at the small finance banks are the thinnest of any group: 0.69% on assets and 6.38% on equity, down 0.3 points and 1.8 points. Public sector banks still take 57.03% of all deposits in India, 0.6 points less than a year earlier.
