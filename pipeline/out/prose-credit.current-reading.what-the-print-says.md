Scheduled commercial banks' credit grew 19.08% in August 2026 from a year earlier, against 10.03% in August 2025, and non-food credit grew 18.77%. All four big sectors ran faster than a year ago: agriculture at 17.22%, industry at 18.23%, services at 24.35% and personal loans at 16.92%, against 7.59%, 7.02%, 10.27% and 11.85% in August 2025. Among loan types, home loans grew 11.13%, loans to non-banking financial companies 37.47% against 3.71% a year earlier, and loans to public financial institutions 83.71%.

Food credit, the small seasonal channel that funds grain procurement, grew 145.81%, faster than anything else, but it follows the procurement calendar rather than the credit cycle. Loans against gold jewellery grew 83.23%, down from 130.19% a year earlier, a seventh straight month of slowing. Credit cards grew 3.64% against 4.38% a year earlier, education loans 13.16% against 14.58%, transport operators 8.63% against 9.67% and other services 15.37% against 16.40%: four more categories moving against a total that rose. The amount lent rose over the month in 36 of the 43 credit categories and in 30 of the 43 industries.
