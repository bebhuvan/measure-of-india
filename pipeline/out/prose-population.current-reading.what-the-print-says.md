The UN's estimate for 2023 puts India at 143.81 crore people on 1 July, up 1.27 crore from 142.54 crore a year earlier. That is growth of 0.9%, barely different from the year before, and the parts beneath it are pulling apart: births fell for a fifth year to 23.2 million, deaths rose 1.4% to 9.5 million, and about 979,000 more people left the country than arrived.

Each woman had 1.98 children on average, below the level that replaces a generation, and that figure has been falling for 59 years. The median Indian is 28.06 years old, up 0.38 years in one year. Life expectancy at birth is 72.00 years, itself up 0.30 in a year, with women at 73.60 and men at 70.52. Just 6.92% of Indians are 65 or older. The number of children aged 5 to 9 is falling too, down 1.3 million in a year.
