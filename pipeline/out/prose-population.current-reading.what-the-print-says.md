On 1 July 2023 the UN estimates India's population at 1.4 billion, 0.9% more than a year earlier. Births were about 23.2 million and deaths about 9.5 million, leaving a natural increase of 13.7 million. Migration ran the other way: more people left the country than arrived. Indians aged 65 and over were 6.9% of the population.

A woman had 2.0 children on average, below the 2.1 that replaces a generation, and that rate has fallen for 59 straight years, since 1964. The median Indian was 28.1 years old. A baby born in 2023 could expect to live 72.0 years.
