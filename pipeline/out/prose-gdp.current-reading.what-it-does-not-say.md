The 7.82% for Q1 FY27 is an average, not a description of anyone's year: it says nothing about distribution, per-capita growth is lower once population is counted, and the informal economy, which is estimated rather than measured, is not in the quarterly number at all. Consumption grew 7.14% while investment grew 11.95%, GVA at 8.23% ran above the headline so the gap is a net-tax drag rather than a production story, and inside the industry accounts the spread runs from finance and real estate at 12.11% to mining and quarrying at -2.44%. In current prices the economy grew 10.33%, against a deflator of 2.33%, and on the expenditure side exports grew 12.05% while imports contracted 1.1%. The annual accounts, where gross saving is 34.75% of GDP and capital formation grew 8.11%, arrive on a slower clock, and this is only the first estimate of the quarter: the final figure may look different. We do not know how different.
