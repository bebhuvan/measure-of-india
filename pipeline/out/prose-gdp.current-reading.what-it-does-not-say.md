It does not say the average Indian is 7.8% better off in Q1 FY27: GDP counts the total, not who received it, and the informal economy behind it is estimated rather than measured. This is the first estimate of the quarter, so the 7.8% will be revised; by how much, we do not know. The headline also hides a wide spread: among the eight industry groups, growth runs from 12.1% in finance and real estate down to -2.4% in mining and quarrying, and utilities, at 8.9%, is the rate that sped up most from a year earlier. Within the quarter, investment grew 12.0%, a fourth straight rise, and GVA 8.2%; consumption was the weakest of the four at 7.1%, and exports grew 12.1% while imports shrank 1.1%. Nominal GDP grew 10.3% and the deflator was 2.3%; the annual accounts put gross saving at 34.8% of GDP.
