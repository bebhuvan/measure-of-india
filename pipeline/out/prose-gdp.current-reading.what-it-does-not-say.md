The headline number is 8.23% for Q2 FY26, up from 7.81% last quarter. It does not say the average Indian is 8.23% better off — GDP says nothing about distribution, and per-capita growth is lower once population is counted. It says nothing reliable about the informal economy, which is estimated, not measured. And unlike the CPI, this number will be revised — this is the first estimate of this quarter, and the final figure, due in about three years, may look different. We do not yet know how different.
