August 2026 sat 0.63°C above India's 1991-2020 average for the month, at a country average of 26.99°C. It is the second warmest of the 87 Augusts since 1940, short of the warmest. The level has fallen for three straight prints, since May. The anomaly is a different series, and it moved the other way: up 0.07°C from July, even as the thermometer itself dropped. Seven of the eight months of 2026 so far ran above their own normal, and the year to date averages 0.45°C above it. The last twelve months average 0.24°C above normal; the annual cuts, a step behind, put 2025 at 24.23°C and 1,404.35 mm of rain. One month is weather, and it does not tell us how the year will end.
