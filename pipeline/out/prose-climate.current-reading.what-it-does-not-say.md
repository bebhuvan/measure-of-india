August 2026 came in 0.63°C above the same month's 1991-2020 average. That baseline sits after decades of warming: the station record puts the century's change at 0.87°C, so a month at what passes for normal is not a month out of a cooler past. The national average also blurs the country, since states can move in opposite directions, days and nights by different amounts, dry heat and humid heat pressed into one number. The level itself, 26.99°C, is seasonal, comparable with other Augusts and not with July. Recent months are revised as more observations enter the reanalysis, and the annual figure for 2025 sits at 24.23°C. We do not know from one month whether the year will end warm or cool.
