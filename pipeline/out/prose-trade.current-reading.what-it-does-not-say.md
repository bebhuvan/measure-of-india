These are goods, not services: in July 2026 India exported $44.3 billion and imported $76.2 billion, leaving a deficit of $31.9 billion.

A dollar value mixes price and volume: a rise in fuel or gold imports can be dearer oil or dearer gold, not more of either.

Growth on a year earlier depends on that month too; exports were up 19.8% and imports 17.4%.

Underneath the totals, refined fuel and electronics lead exports, electronics and fuel again lead imports, aircraft falls furthest on the import side, and by partner the surplus with the United States is $3.6 billion against a deficit with China of $12.5 billion.

The first estimate for August 2026 puts exports at $43.8 billion, provisional and subject to revision.
