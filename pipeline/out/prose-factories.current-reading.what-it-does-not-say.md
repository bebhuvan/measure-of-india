The 2023-24 release puts gross value added up 11.9%, and the number needs a warning attached. It is nominal: part of any rise in value is more output, part is dearer output, and this release does not split the two. We do not know how much is volume. The survey covers registered factories: 2.60 lakh of them, 1.96 crore people engaged. The unincorporated workshops beside them are a separate instrument, never summed with this one, so a total that adds the two is not from these tables. Even inside the registered universe the year is uneven, with food products' output growing while coke and refined petroleum fell, and these numbers arrive about two years after the year they describe, so the present is not in them.
