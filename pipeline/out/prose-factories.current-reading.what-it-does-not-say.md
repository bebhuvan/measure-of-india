It does not say Indian manufacturing is formalising. Gross value added in registered factories was ₹24.6 lakh crore in 2023-24, up 11.9% on the year before, but that figure is nominal: part of the rise is prices, and the return does not say which part. Gross output reached ₹153.3 lakh crore, up 5.8%, while factories in operation rose 3.1%, to about 2.1 lakh. The informal counterpart is a separate instrument: roughly 12 crore workers in unincorporated enterprises, counted by another survey entirely, never added to the factory tally. The print also lands with a lag of about two years. We do not know what the factory floor looks like this month.
