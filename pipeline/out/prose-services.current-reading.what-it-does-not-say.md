Services were 54.03% of the economy's value added in FY26, by the provisional estimate. That is a share of output, not of jobs: the jobs share comes from the labour force survey, and the two have never been expected to match. The monthly Index of Services Production is still a trial. It covers only the formal part of services, carries no overall figure, and its components pull apart: administrative and support services up 20.8% year on year, air transport down 8.5%. Trade in services is counted every month, $33.4 billion of exports in the latest month, but the annual figures will be revised as later returns arrive. How fast the informal service business grows from year to year, we do not know: the survey that estimates the corner shop runs a year or more behind.
