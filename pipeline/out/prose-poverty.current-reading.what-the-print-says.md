In 2023-24, 2.6% of Indians lived below $3.00 a day, down from 5.3% in 2022-23. That is about 37 million people. At $4.20 a day the share is 16.1%, down from 23.9%; at $8.30 it is 80.0%, down from 82.1%. The choice of line decides the answer.

The count below $4.20 a day fell by about 109 million, to 233 million. The estimates rest on about 1.4 billion people, 0.9% more than a year earlier. Consumption grew more even: the Gini index fell 2.5 points to 23, the richest tenth's share slipped to 20.5% and the poorest tenth's rose to 4.9%.
