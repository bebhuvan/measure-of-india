In Q1 2026 Indian borrowers owed 188.6% of a year's GDP, 8.4 points more than a year earlier. Private credit is the line moving most: 105.1%, up 9.1 points, with the credit-to-GDP gap above its long-run trend at 4.3 points, itself up 9.3 points in a year. Both are BIS estimates, not observations: since the RBI's last financial accounts, for Q4 2023, BIS extends private credit with bank credit and holds the household and company split fixed. So the last measured figures are 43.8% of GDP for households and 50.0% for companies, both for Q4 2023, and nothing in this release says which way either has moved since. General government debt is measured: 83.5%, down 0.7 points on a year ago and lower for a ninth straight print since Q4 2023, the one series pulling against the total. Debt service takes 12.5% of private income, up 0.8 points on a year earlier. On the same definitions Chinese companies owed 146.6% of GDP and Chinese households 57.5%, against India's last measured 43.8%, while American households, at 67.2%, owe more than Indian ones.
