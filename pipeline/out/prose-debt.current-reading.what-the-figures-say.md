In Q1 2026 all Indian borrowers together owed 188.6% of GDP, up 8.4 points on a year earlier. Households owed 49.1% and companies 56%, both more than a year ago. Private borrowers as a whole moved furthest: at 105.1% they have risen for six straight quarters, since Q3 2024. General government went the other way. Its 83.5% is down 0.7 points on the year, the ninth consecutive decline, so the total is climbing on private borrowing alone. The credit gap, private credit less its long-run trend, is 4.3 points, and the debt service ratio is 12.5% of income. China's companies, by comparison, owe 146.6% of GDP.
