These are counts of payments, not of people. Nothing in the table says how many Indians use UPI, or how often each one pays: a barber who takes a hundred scans in a day adds a hundred to July 2026's total and leaves that question exactly where it was. Every average here is a mean, and a mean is pulled up by the few large transfers, so the average UPI payment of ₹1,263 in July 2026 describes nobody in particular, and we do not know what the ordinary payment looks like because the RBI publishes no typical one. Cash payments are not counted at all, and the cash people hold kept growing: currency with the public was ₹42.1 lakh crore on 15 September 2026, up 13.6% from a year earlier. Values count the same rupee every time it moves, so nothing here is spending, income or GDP, and a count can swing on reasons that have nothing to do with how the country pays. The most recent month can be revised.
