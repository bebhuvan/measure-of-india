Bank credit grew 19.1% in August 2026, and that figure is a stock: what banks were owed at month-end, not what they lent that month. A loan repaid and a loan made can leave the total exactly where it was. Nothing in the number says whether the money comes back; that shows up in bad-loan figures a year or two later, and not in this one at all. The sector split covers selected banks rather than every bank, and the lending that non-banking financial companies do with their own money sits outside it. One more caveat is arithmetic. Since December 2025 the year-on-year rate compares a month-end with an older reporting Friday, which flatters growth by an amount we do not know.
