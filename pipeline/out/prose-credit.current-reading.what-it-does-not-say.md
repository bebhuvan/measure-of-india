Bank credit outstanding in August 2026 was 19.08% higher than a year earlier, and that measures a stock, not a flow. It does not tell you how much fresh lending took place: a repayment and a new loan of the same size cancel out. Nothing here says whether the loans will be repaid; that shows up in bad-loan figures a year or two later. The sector detail comes from selected banks, not all of them, and the lending of non-banking financial companies sits outside it. Since December 2025 the year-on-year rate compares a month-end with an older reporting Friday, which flatters it. By how much, we do not know.
