Bank credit was 19.08% higher in August 2026 than a year earlier. That is a comparison of two stocks, not a count of new lending: a loan repaid and a loan made can leave the total where it stood. Repayment is a different question, answered later in bad-loan figures. The sector detail comes from selected banks, not all of them, and non-banking financial companies' own lending sits outside the totals even as bank credit to those companies grew 37.47%. Since December 2025 the year-on-year rate compares a month-end with an older reporting Friday, and it flatters the number by an amount we do not know. The lines underneath disagree in any case: gold loans are up 83.23% on a year earlier but down 4.9 points from July, credit cards at 3.64% are below their 4.38% of a year ago, and transport operators and education loans have slipped as well.
