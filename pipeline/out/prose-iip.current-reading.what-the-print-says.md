Industrial growth was 7.97% in Aug 2026, against 7.35% in July and 4.67% a year earlier. Manufacturing, the biggest weight, grew 8.95%; electricity grew 12.33%, 3.6 points faster than July. Capital goods, the line that tracks investment, ran highest at 16.86%, 10.9 points above a year earlier, though slower than July's 19.00%. Mining was the exception, and the weakest line: it contracted 5.62%, against growth of 15.82% a year earlier. Not every line joined in: water supply at 6.34%, infrastructure and construction goods at 6.43% and primary goods at 3.52% each grew more slowly than a year earlier. Consumer durables grew 11.07% and intermediate goods 13.72%; consumer non-durables, the everyday basket, grew 2.05%, after contracting in July. Underneath, the thirty-two industries range from electrical equipment at 30.92% down to non-metallic minerals at -12.76%, the median industry grew 8.62%, and 17 accelerated against July while 15 slowed and eight contracted.
