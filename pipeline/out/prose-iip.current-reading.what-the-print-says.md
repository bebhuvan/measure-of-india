Industrial production rose 7.97% in Aug 2026, from 7.35% in July and 4.67% a year earlier. Manufacturing and electricity carried the advance, at 8.95% and 12.33%. Capital goods was the standout among the broad lines at 16.86%, the line usually read as the investment cycle's pulse. Mining went the other way: it fell 5.62%, 21.44 points below a year ago, and was the weakest of the four sectors. Other lines lagged the headline too, among them infrastructure and construction goods at 6.43%, water supply at 6.34% and primary goods at 3.52%. The 32-industry detail ranged from electrical equipment at 30.92% to non-metallic minerals at -12.76%, with a median of 8.6% and 17 industries rising against 15 falling. We do not know yet how much of mining's fall is one-off.
