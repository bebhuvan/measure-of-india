Industrial production grew 7.32% in June, its third straight rise and the fastest since July 2024. Manufacturing did the heavy lifting at 7.78%, while electricity and gas ran at 10.64%. Mining, the laggard, managed just 1.01%. The investment signal is the clearest: capital goods — machines being made for factories — grew 14.23%, though that is down from May's 15.53%. The acceleration is genuine, but part of it is arithmetic. A year ago the index grew only 2.23%, so this June's number climbs off a low step. The flattery expires with the next base.
