In FY25 the richest state's income per person was ₹6,46,183 and the poorest's ₹69,211, a multiple of 9.34. That is down from 9.41 a year earlier, a fourth straight year of narrowing. The narrowest reading the panel shows is older and lower: 8.46 in FY14, below today's level, with the present run of declines dating only to FY21. The narrowing is genuine, then, but it is small and recent. Across all states the spread closed a little: measured by the coefficient of variation, it was 49.6% in FY25, down 0.1 points from 49.7%. One measure runs the other way, on constant prices the richest-poorest multiple widened to 9.44 from 9.01. The typical state's economy grew 7.4% in real terms, down 0.4 points from 7.8%, while the median state's income per person was ₹2,58,196, up 10.1%; the richest's rose 10.4% and the poorest's 11.3%.
