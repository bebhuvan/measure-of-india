In FY25 the richest state's income per person was ₹6,46,183 and the poorest's ₹69,211, so the richest stood at 9.34 times the poorest. That multiple has now fallen for four prints running. The cooling is genuine but small: seven hundredths of a point below FY24's 9.41. It has not been a steady drift, though: the multiple's low came early, at 8.46 in FY14, below today's level, and it climbed again before it turned down. At constant prices the same multiple moved the other way, up 0.43 points to 9.44, which contradicts the current-price headline. Across all states the spread narrowed barely at all, from 49.68% to 49.63% on the coefficient of variation, five hundredths of a point. The typical state's economy grew 7.35% in real terms, down 0.48 points from 7.83% the year before.
