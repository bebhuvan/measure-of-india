This is the first estimate for Q1 FY27, and it is a draft: the RBI revises these numbers as more data arrive. One quarter is not a year: over the full fiscal year 2025-26 the current account deficit was 0.6% of GDP. In the quarter itself goods stayed in deficit at $86.1 billion and invisibles in surplus at $81.8 billion, with gross current payments of $299.2 billion. The reserves have already moved since the quarter closed: $747.6 billion in the week to 25 September, below the peak of $785.7 billion on 4 September. Some of that is valuation, not trading: gold and other currencies change what the reserves are worth in dollars, and the rupee's real effective exchange rate on a broad basket, base 2015-16, was 92 in August. We do not know how far the revision will move the quarter's numbers, or what the September quarter will show.
