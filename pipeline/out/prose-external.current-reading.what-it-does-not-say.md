Q1 FY27 is one quarter, and its account is a first estimate. The current account was in deficit by $4.2 billion, after a surplus of $6.5 billion in the quarter before: goods ran a deficit of $86.1 billion, and invisibles earned $81.8 billion against it. These numbers get revised, sometimes by billions. One quarter is not a year: through 2025-26 the current account deficit was 0.61% of GDP. Reserves have moved since the quarter ended, to $747.6 billion in the week of 25 September from a peak of $785.7 billion on 4 September, and only part of that is the RBI's trading: gold and other currencies change price too. We do not know what the revision will show, or how the current quarter is going.
