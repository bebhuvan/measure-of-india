On the new 2024 base, headline inflation opened 2026 at 2.7%. By August 2026 it was 4.8%, a seventh straight rise and 0.8 points above the 4% target: still inside the band, but no longer near its floor. Food and beverages has climbed with it, to 5.7% from 2.1% in January. The spread from the hottest division to the coldest is 13.9 points. At the bottom, health sits at 1.3% and has been under the 2% floor for a seventh print; at the top, personal care runs at 15.2%, its eighth print above the 6% ceiling, though its price is set in world markets, not here. The line to watch is food.
