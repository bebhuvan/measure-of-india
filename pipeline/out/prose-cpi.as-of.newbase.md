The new base's first half-year is one long climb. Headline inflation opened January at 2.73% and now stands at 4.38% in June — five straight monthly rises, the longest run of the year. That puts the print 0.38 points above the RBI's 4% target, inside the tolerance band but no longer flattering it. The climb is broad: food rose from 2.12% to 5.06%, and transport jumped 2.56 points in a single month. Watch whether the June reading is the top of the arc or a way station. The arithmetic of the base effect is not yet doing the work; this is demand, not a gift.
