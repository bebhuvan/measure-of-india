This is a factory-gate index, and the 9.92% headline for August 2026 stops at the gate. No rents, no school fees, no services of any kind, and retail margins sit between this number and your receipt. Underneath the headline the readings run from fuel at 22.93% down to food at 7.05%, a spread of 15.88 points, with primary articles at 7.76% and manufactured products at 8.37% between them. Deeper still the range widens: mineral oils at 38.48% against electricity at -1.73%, sulphur at 303.87% against tomato at -40.2%, and 16 of the 142 sub-groups and 141 of the 953 items below zero. Pass-through takes months, not weeks, to reach the shop. The figure is also provisional, revised to a final number about eight weeks from now, and we do not know how large that revision will be.
