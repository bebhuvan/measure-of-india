Wholesale prices were 9.92% higher in Aug 2026 than a year earlier, and this is a factory-gate number: rents, school fees and every service sit outside it, and shop margins stand between it and your receipt. Fuel sped up most against a year earlier, to 22.93%, and manufactured products moved least, to 8.37%; primary articles stood at 7.76% and food at 7.05%. Energy takes months, not weeks, to reach a shop, and we do not know how much of the fuel rise gets there. The figure is provisional, revised in about eight weeks; the revision is usually small, occasionally not. The spread underneath is wider than the headline suggests: Mineral Oils is highest of the 29 two-digit groups at 38.48% and Electricity lowest at -1.73%, while the 953 items run from sulphur at 303.87% to tomato at -40.2%. Of the 142 sub-groups, 82 rose faster than the month before and 59 slower, with Lignite at 62.42% and Manufacture Of Magnetic And Optical Media at -14.59% at the ends; of the eight sub-sub-groups, Fruits is highest at 8.18% and the lowest grade of coal at -7.82%.
