The headline rate for August 2026 is 9.92%, and it does not say your cost of living rose in step: this index stops at the factory gate, with no rents, no school fees, no services at all, and retail margins sitting between this number and your receipt. Fuel moved the most, up 2.9 points to 22.9%. That is below the 30.8% it reached in May, and energy takes months, not weeks, to reach consumer prices. Below that the basket is not one story: primary at 7.8%, food at 7.1%, and manufactured at 8.4%, a rate that has now risen for nine prints running; among the 953 items the median is 5.9%, and 141 sit below zero. The spread widens further down: the two-digit groups run from Mineral Oils at 38.5% to Electricity at -1.7%, the sub-groups from Lignite at 62.4% to magnetic and optical media at -14.6%, the individual items from Sulphur at 303.9% to Tomato at -40.2%, and the eight sub-sub-groups from Fruits at 8.2% to non-coking coal at -7.8%. It is provisional, revised about eight weeks from now, and whether that spread narrows we do not know.
