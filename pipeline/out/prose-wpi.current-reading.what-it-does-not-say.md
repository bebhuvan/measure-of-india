The headline reading of 8.36% for April is the highest in this run, and the run itself is six prints long. But the number does not say your cost of living jumped in step. This index stops at the factory gate: no rents, no school fees, no services of any kind, and retail margins sit between this number and your receipt. What changed arrived through the energy pipe, and energy takes months, not weeks, to reach consumer prices. And it is provisional — revised to a final figure roughly eight weeks from now; the revision is usually small, and occasionally not. We do not know yet which way this one will move.
