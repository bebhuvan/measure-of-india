GDP grew 7.8% in Q1 FY27, 0.9 points above the 6.9% of a year earlier; nominal GDP grew 10.3%, against a deflator of 2.3%. The last four quarters read 8.1%, 7.7%, 8.6% and 7.8%, a flat run that sits 1.8 points above the rough 6% pre-pandemic trend. These are early estimates, and revisions will redraw parts of the picture. Among the four headline series, investment is fastest at 12.0%, up 6.2 points on a year earlier and higher for a fourth straight print; GVA grew 8.2%, GDP 7.8%, and consumption is slowest at 7.1%, up 0.3 points on a year earlier. Exports ran at 12.1% while imports fell 1.1%; among the eight industry groups, finance and real estate led at 12.1%, mining was lowest at -2.4%, and utilities sped up most, to 8.9%. In the annual accounts, the gross saving rate is 34.8% of GDP and capital formation grew 8.1%.
