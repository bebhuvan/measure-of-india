The last four quarters read 6.82, 8.35, 7.96, 7.83 — a peak in Q2 FY26, then two straight prints of cooling. Growth still sits 1.83 points above the old 6% trend, and it has now stayed above that line for twelve consecutive quarters. The dip is real but shallow: the latest print is 0.13 points off the previous one, and still 0.85 points above a year ago. The caution is the usual one. These are early estimates, leaning on listed-company results and government accounts; the informal sector is extrapolated. Revisions will redraw parts of this picture.
