Q1 FY27 GDP came in at 7.82%, down 0.7 points from Q4's 8.56%; the last four quarters read 8.1, 7.7, 8.6, 7.8, still 1.8 points above the rough 6% pre-pandemic trend. Investment has moved up four prints running since Q1 FY26, to 11.95%, 6.2 points above a year ago. Consumption is the slowest of the four headline series at 7.14%, just 0.4 points above its year-ago reading; GVA grew 8.23%, above the headline. The industry detail runs from finance and real estate, up 12.11%, to mining and quarrying, down 2.44%, with utilities the biggest gain on a year ago at 8.89%; on the expenditure side, exports grew 12.05% and imports fell 1.1%. In the annual accounts, the gross saving rate is 34.75% of GDP and subsidies, capital formation and capital consumption grew 7.73%, 8.11% and 6.25%; nominal GDP grew 10.33% against a deflator of 2.33%. These are early estimates, and revisions will redraw parts of this picture.
