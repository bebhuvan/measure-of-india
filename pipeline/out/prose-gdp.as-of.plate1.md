The last four quarters read 6.37, 7.38, 7.81, 8.23 — a clean, accelerating climb that puts Q2 FY26 at 2.23 points above the old 6% trend. The run is four quarters long and unbroken, the strongest signal since before the pandemic. The caution: these are early estimates. The quarterly series leans on listed-company results and government accounts; the informal sector is extrapolated. Revisions over the next three years will redraw parts of this picture.
