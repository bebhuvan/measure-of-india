The last four quarters read 8.1, 7.7, 8.6, 7.8. Q1 FY27 closes at 7.82%, which is 1.8 points above the 6% trend line. Investment is doing the work: 11.95%, up 6.2 points on a year ago and rising for a fourth straight quarter, while consumption is the slowest of the four at 7.14%, up 0.4 points, and GVA at 8.23% runs ahead of the headline. The spread underneath is wider than the headline suggests: exports at 12.05% against imports at -1.1%, finance and real estate at 12.11% against mining at -2.44%, and utilities the fastest riser at 8.89%. In the annual accounts, gross saving stands at 34.75% of GDP and capital consumption grew 6.25%, with subsidies the biggest riser at 7.73% and capital formation the biggest faller at 8.11%; nominal GDP of 10.33% sits over a deflator of 2.33%. The caution is the standing one: these are early estimates, and revisions will move them.
