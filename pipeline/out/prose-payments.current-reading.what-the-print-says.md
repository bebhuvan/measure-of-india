UPI carried 2,366 crore payments in July 2026, 21.5% more than in the same month a year earlier, and moved ₹29.9 lakh crore, up 19.1%. That was 87.0% of the 2,719 crore retail payments made that month, and the average UPI payment was ₹1,263, below the ₹1,289 of July 2025.

Wallet and prepaid card payments grew fastest of the fourteen channels counted here, up 29.9%; credit card payments rose 24.5% while debit cards fell 9.4%. Across all card payments, credit cards outnumber debit cards six to one; at shops' card machines, four to one. IMPS fell 24.4%, cheques and other paper 9.7%, cash withdrawals at ATMs 8.9%, cash withdrawals at micro ATMs 3.2% and APBS benefit credits 1.4%, while NACH debits, the EMIs and bills, rose 10.2%. NACH credits, salaries and pensions, fell 26.5%, the weakest of them. RTGS and NEFT still carry the money: ₹223.1 lakh crore and ₹46.6 lakh crore, or 69.5% and 14.5% of the value of all payments.
