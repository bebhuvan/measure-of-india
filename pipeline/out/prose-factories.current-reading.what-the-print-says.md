In 2023-24 the registered factory sector added ₹24.6 lakh crore of gross value added, 11.9% more than the year before. Factories in operation rose 3.1% to 2,12,990, and the register grew 2.7% to 2,60,061 factories. Gross output rose 5.8%, to about ₹153.3 lakh crore, on inputs up 4.7%; fixed capital rose 12.2% and invested capital 10.8%. Emoluments rose 11.8%, persons engaged 5.9%, workers 6.2%. The registered frame is the smaller half: the unincorporated survey, in its 2023-24 round, counts 12.1 crore workers, several times the factory headcount. Its calendar-year 2025 round, January to December, counts 12.8 crore workers. That is a different twelve months from the 2023-24 round, and no change between the two can be read off.
