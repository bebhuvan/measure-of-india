The $3.00 a day line is the World Bank's, not India's: in 2023, 2.6% of Indians lived below it. India has had no official poverty line since 2011-12, and the survey measures what households spend, not what they earn or own. The richest homes rarely answer it. The 233 million people below $4.20 a day in 2023 are a headcount applied to a projected population of about 1.4 billion, and the years between surveys are World Bank model estimates. The redesigned questionnaire is not comparable with 2011-12, so part of the fall between the two rounds is the new questionnaire. The World Bank publishes no separate rural and urban counts for India, so we do not know how the poorest households in the countryside fared.
