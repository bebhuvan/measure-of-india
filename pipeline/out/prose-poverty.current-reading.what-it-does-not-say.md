By the World Bank's $3.00 line, 2.57% of Indians were below it in 2023; by its $4.20 line, 233.0 million were. Neither line is India's own. India has published no official poverty line since 2011-12, so the threshold does much of the work. The survey records what households consume, not what they earn or own, and it rarely reaches the richest homes, so nothing here measures wealth. The redesigned questionnaire is not comparable with the 2011-12 round, so part of the fall across that gap is the new ruler, not a new country, and the years in between are the Bank's model estimates, issued as one national figure for 1.4 billion people, with no rural or urban split. We do not know how much of the decline belongs to the questionnaire and how much to the economy.
