What these figures measure is production per resident, not what a family takes home. A worker who crosses a state border is counted where they work, so the states that draw them in look richer and the states they leave look poorer. Each state counts once, whatever its size. In FY25 the richest state produced about 9.3 times as much per person as the poorest. In rupees the distance between them kept growing even as the multiple narrowed, and the latest year is an estimate each state will revise. Whether living standards have converged as much as production has, we do not know.
