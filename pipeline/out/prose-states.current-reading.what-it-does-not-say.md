Production per resident is not take-home pay. Income per person here is what a state makes divided by the people who live in it, so a worker who crosses a border for a job, or sends money home from one, is counted where they work and not where that money is spent. States that receive workers look richer by this measure, states that send them look poorer, and nothing in these tables corrects for it. Each state counts once, whatever its size. The FY25 figures are estimates each state will revise. The multiple at the top of this page, 9.34, can keep shrinking while the rupees behind it stretch further apart, and whether living standards have converged as much as output has, we do not know from these numbers.
