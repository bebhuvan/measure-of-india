The Centre's fiscal deficit ran to ₹4,55,144 crore from April to July 2026, 26.84% of the year's budget estimate. At the same point a year earlier it stood at 29.86% of that year's estimate, so the deficit is running 3.0 points lower.

Tax revenue, net of the states' share, has brought in 29.46% of the year's budget estimate against 23.32% a year earlier, and total receipts excluding borrowing stand at 35.79% of the year's budget estimate against 31.32%. Total expenditure has used 32.95% of the year's budget estimate against 30.87% a year earlier, and capital expenditure is further along at 36.88% against 30.95%. Interest payments are the one large item behind: 30.38% of the year's budget estimate against 35.00% a year earlier. Three smaller lines of receipts also lag their year-earlier shares: non-tax revenue, non-debt capital receipts and other receipts. The revenue deficit is ₹43,645 crore against ₹1,51,279 crore a year earlier, 7.37% of the year's budget estimate against 28.88%.
