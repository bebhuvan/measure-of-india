From April to July 2026 the Centre ran a fiscal deficit of ₹4,55,144 crore, which is 26.8% of the year's budget estimate. At the same point a year earlier the deficit stood at 29.9% of that year's estimate, so this year's running total is 3.1 points lower.

Receipts are doing most of the work: tax revenue, net of the states' share, had reached 29.5% of the year's budget estimate by July, against 23.3% a year earlier, and total receipts, excluding borrowing, stood at 35.8% of the year's budget estimate against 31.3%. Total expenditure was 33.0% of the year's budget estimate against 30.9%, with capital expenditure at 36.9% against 31.0% and interest payments, which fall due on a fixed calendar, at 30.4% against 35.0%. Non-tax revenue ran behind, at 63.5% of the year's budget estimate against 69.2%, and other receipts, mainly disinvestment, at 34.5% against 48.2%. The revenue deficit is the year's weakest mover: ₹43,645 crore so far, against ₹1,51,279 crore a year earlier. Further back, of the 48 series that follow the Centre's fourteen headline items from budget estimate to final accounts, 41 are rising and 7 falling.
